# Product Brief

## Working title
CheerPracticePlayer

## Positioning
Programmable practice playback for cheer teams.

## Problem
Coaches waste practice time manually scrubbing music, tracking rests, re-cueing sections, and trying to clean timing with no dedicated tool.

## Core promise
Run practice from the team's actual mix with programmable section repeats, timed breaks, lead-ins, and sync support.

## Primary user
All-star cheer coach or choreographer running team practices.

## Prototype success criteria
- Coaches understand the product in under 30 seconds
- A practice block can be configured in under 2 minutes
- Live mode feels easier than manual playback
- At least 3 real coaches say they would use it with their own mix
