Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

Summary: CheerPracticePlayer does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything stays on your device.

What CheerPracticePlayer stores

The app stores the following data locally in its app container:

  • Audio files you import via the system Files picker (copied to the app)
  • Section markers (time ranges within your imported mix)
  • Practice block settings (reps, rest seconds, lead-in seconds, restart mode)
  • Saved mix templates

This data is stored in your device's secure application container and never transmitted off your device.

What is NOT collected

  • No email addresses or contact information
  • No names or user accounts
  • No photos or videos
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
  • No usage analytics
  • No crash reports sent to third parties

Third-party services

CheerPracticePlayer does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or SDKs. The app does not communicate with any servers.

Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+ and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
