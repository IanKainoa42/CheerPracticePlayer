# CheerPracticePlayer Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 19, 2026

## Overview

CheerPracticePlayer is a practice-mode audio player for cheer coaches. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app stores and how it's handled.

**The short version:** All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or share any information.

---

## Data Collection

### What Data Is Stored

CheerPracticePlayer stores the following information locally on your device:

**Imported Audio**
- Audio files you import from the Files app (copied to the app's container)

**Practice Templates**
- Section markers (time ranges within your mix)
- Practice blocks (reps, rest seconds, lead-in seconds, restart mode)
- Saved mix templates

### What Data Is NOT Collected

CheerPracticePlayer does **not** collect:
- Email addresses or contact information
- Names, accounts, or user identifiers
- Photos or videos
- Location data
- Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV)
- Usage analytics
- Crash reports

---

## Data Storage

All data is stored **locally on your device** in the app's secure container.

- Audio files and templates are stored under Application Support
- Data is protected by your device's passcode / biometrics
- Data is included in your iCloud / iTunes backups (if enabled)
- Data is deleted when you uninstall the app

**We do not operate servers.** Your data never leaves your device.

---

## Data Sharing

**We do not share your data with anyone.**

- No data is transmitted over the internet
- No third-party analytics services
- No advertising networks
- No cloud synchronization
- No third-party SDKs

Your data exists only on your device.

---

## Device Permissions

CheerPracticePlayer does not request any sensitive permissions. You select audio files directly via the system Files picker — the app receives only the files you choose.

---

## Children's Privacy

CheerPracticePlayer does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content.
